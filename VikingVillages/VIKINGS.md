# Vikings

There are four kinds of Viking across Viking Villages and Zombie Apocalypse. This compares them - each mod's README has the full details.

PC and NPC Vikings live in villages the way you live in the world. PC Vikings fight for their village and go adventuring for it, while NPC Vikings are its peaceful villagers, who keep out of fights and see to its chores. Dvergr Vikings work for the Dvergr, and zombie Vikings are the dead.

## Eating

Only PC and NPC Vikings eat, and both eat the same way. Dvergr and zombie Vikings never eat - their health is set by their biome and stars and never changes.

A new PC or NPC Viking arrives having just eaten their biome's three foods and carrying 5-6 more, and a Dvergr Viking carries 0-2 of the Mistlands' foods they never eat (see [Starting Food](PROGRESSION.md#starting-food)).

### What food does for them

Food works for a PC or NPC Viking the way it works for you:

- Their max health is a base of 25, plus the health of each food they've eaten. It fades as each food wears off.
- They can have up to three different foods at once, and each lasts as long as it would for you.
- Food is the only thing that heals them. It heals every 10 seconds, faster while they're resting or rested, so a Viking who hasn't eaten doesn't heal at all.
- Food also gives them stamina on top of your base stamina, which they only use for swimming.

A village can't make every food you can, so their max health never drops below what the best three foods of the biome before theirs would give (note - this will be removed when farms are implemented). That's 50 in the Meadows, rising to 325 in the Deep North. Their own biome's best food takes them past that, and better food you leave in their chests takes them further while it lasts (see [Eating](LOGIC.md#eating)).

### When they eat

By day, with no work more urgent, a Viking eats in one of two cases:

1. **When they're hurt** - below three quarters of their health. They go to the nearest chest with food and eat whatever heals fastest. They eat one food at a time while they still need to, up to three, and eat each again once half of it has worn off. When they find nothing they can eat, they look again a minute later (see [Eat](JOBS.md#eat)).
2. **To keep their foods up** - only while the village's chests hold at least three foods for every Viking, so a short village saves its food for the wounded. They eat once a food place is free or a food is down to a tenth of its time. They pick the weakest food that still lifts them above their least max health, which leaves the strong food for the wounded and for fights. With none that weak, they eat the strongest of the rest. At full health they only eat food that raises their max health, so they don't waste it (see [Keep Their Foods Up](JOBS.md#keep-their-foods-up)).

Through a raid, they only eat when they're hurt.

### Running short

While the chests hold fewer than three foods per Viking, they get more:

- They forage and hunt more often.
- With no wild prey about, they slaughter the village's livestock.
- With less than one food per Viking, the village sends out a hunting or foraging quest before anything else (see [Get Food](JOBS.md#get-food) and [How a Quest Is Picked](QUESTS.md#how-a-quest-is-picked)).

NPC Vikings forage and slaughter livestock but never hunt.

### Away from home

- **On a quest:** before setting out, they pack enough food to keep them fed for as long as they'll be gone, up to 9 pieces. That's their strongest food for a quest that fights, and the food they'd keep their foods up on for any other. Out there, their food heals them through the work and on the way home (see [Leave on a Quest](JOBS.md#leave-on-a-quest)).
- **On the road and out of reach:** they eat what they carry the way they would at home, and anything they pick along the way. Their food keeps healing them, so they arrive as fed and healed as their food made them (see [Arriving](LOGIC.md#arriving)).
- **While no player is near:** their food doesn't wear off, and neither does the night you sleep through, so you come back to a village fed the way you left it.
