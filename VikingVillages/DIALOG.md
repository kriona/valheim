# Viking Villages - What Vikings Say

Every line the living Vikings of [Viking Villages](README.md) say, and when they say it - to you, to each other, and as they pass on the news of what you've done.

## Speech

What they say to you, and what they shout about danger, shows over their heads for everyone nearby, and each time one line is picked at random from those that fit. It shows in a speech bubble sized to the line, just above their health bar, following them as they move - with their name over the line when their health bar isn't showing. When the Viking speaking is off screen, the bubble is pinned to the edge of the screen with their name, behind a gold arrow pointing their way. Calls about danger - an enemy spotted, a raid, a shout for help - and the night watch's alarms stay up longest, and nothing else the Viking says covers them while they show. {enemy} is the enemy's name, {player} is your name and {name} is the name you gave the Viking. Lines and talks marked * are about the dead walking, and Vikings only say them with Zombie Apocalypse running and its `RiseChance` above 0 - and those about burning the dead only with its `FireStaysDead` above 0 too, about dead deer with `AnimalsRise` on, about the dead raiding with `VikingRaids` on, and about newcomers rising with `TombstoneZombies` on, whatever its `RiseChance`. {village} is the name you gave the Viking's village, and lines with it are only said in a village with a name. A Viking asleep - in bed, or resting for the night on a chair or the floor - says nothing, but for a mumble when you talk to them in bed and a word as they're woken to keep watch.

## Talks

When two Vikings talk, they take turns saying the lines of one talk, starting with the one who started it - shown under the speaker's health bar for 6 seconds each, rather than over their head - so only within 10 meters, where health bars show, with the bar kept showing while they speak. In these, {name} is the name of the Viking spoken to, left out with the comma beside it when they have none, {other} is another Viking of the village with a name, and {item} is a material they build or craft with that's in the village's chests, picked at random - wood, fine wood, core wood or stone, or anything an arrow they could craft takes, like flint, resin, feathers or bronze, but never a tool or weapon. With no such material in the chests, they talk about something else. {village} is the village's name, and talks about it are among those they might have once it has one. {fallen} is a named Viking of the village who died in the last 7 days - when the village remembers one, a talk is about them 15% of the time, at most once every 15 minutes in a village. Otherwise, when there's another Viking with a name, a talk is about them a quarter of the time, and otherwise about what's going on - the night or dusk, rain, a fight in the last 5 minutes, or the Viking starting the talk being hurt - 60% of the time when something is.
