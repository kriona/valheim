# Viking Villages - Console Commands

Every console command Viking Villages adds, mostly for trying things out and testing - press F5 to open the console. Those that need `devcommands` only run with it on. On a server without the mod none of them work, since no one else there would have what they make (see Multiplayer in the [README](README.md)). The [README](README.md) has the mod itself, and [QUESTS.md](QUESTS.md) the quests the quest command starts.

## Villages

- `kri_vv_spawnvillage [farm]` - needs `devcommands`, and only works in single player or for the host, since only the server places locations. Spawns an abandoned farm centered where you stand - the one named, or one picked at random - and its village is built and set up over the next few seconds, the same as one found in the world. You stay where you are as it goes up, unless it catches you - something built or placed lands on you, the bonfire goes up where you stand, the ground rises over you or you end up fenced in a pen - and then you're moved to the nearest open ground, out of the houses and clear of the fire. The farm is added to the world's plan of its locations unless its zone already has one. It won't spawn one within 68 meters of the edge of the world's start
- `kri_vv_blueprints [reload]` - lists the house blueprints villages are built with and any problems with them, reading them again first with `reload`
- `kri_vv_cart [break|astray]` - needs `devcommands`. Standing in a village, says what its cart is up to - home, outside the village, out of reach, missing or in use - and where it stands. `break` breaks it, dropping what it's made of and what it holds, so its Vikings build another, and `astray` moves it 15 meters out past the village's edge for them to fetch back (see [The Cart](LOGIC.md#the-cart))

## Quests

- `kri_vv_quest` - needs `devcommands`. Standing in a village, shows everything behind its quests - its goal and the next thing it does toward it, the quest it's on, what each Viking on it takes, works with and brings home, whether its food and wood are running out, its treasure, whether it's trapped, the roads to quests it knows and when its last quest came back
- `kri_vv_quest <kind> [item]` - starts a quest of that kind at once, whatever the village wants, however long since its last and whatever the time of day - as long as its biome isn't too easy for the quest, it has the stations and coins the quest needs and it can spare the Vikings. Only the player whose game runs the village can start its quests - in single player, always you
  - The kinds are `Hunt`, `Forage`, `ChopTrees`, `MineCopper`, `MineTin`, `MineSilver`, `RaidCrypts`, `RaidBurialChambers`, `RaidFrostCaves`, `HuntGhosts`, `HuntTrolls`, `HuntGoblins`, `KillEikthyr`, `FindBees`, `SellTreasure` and `BuyCirclet`, in any case - and a kind that isn't one lists them
  - `item` is what it's for, by its prefab name in any case, and the quest is after 20 of it - `kri_vv_quest ChopTrees FineWood` or `kri_vv_quest Hunt DeerHide`, say. With none, it goes for the first thing the quest brings back that the village can go for. An item the quest doesn't bring back lists the ones it does
  - The first time for a quest, the village asks the server for the road, and the quest starts on its own as soon as the server answers, saying so in the console and at the top left of the screen - or is given up on after 30 seconds
- `kri_vv_quest due` - brings the village's quest home now, once its Vikings have left - only in single player or for the host, whose game keeps them

## Travel and Boats

- `kri_vv_travel <village name|random> [count] [sim]` - needs `devcommands`. Sends the living Viking you're looking at, or else `count` Vikings of the village you're in (1 by default, nearest you first), off to the village named - any village whose name holds what you typed, whatever its biome - or one picked at random, as a group. They leave their village and travel the way any group on the road does, in the world while you're near, so you can follow them, or straight off the world with `sim`
- `kri_vv_spawnboat <Raft|Karve|VikingShip|VikingShip_Ashlands> [full|max|vikings]` - needs `devcommands`. Spawns one of the boats the Vikings build, by its prefab ID - or by its name, like `longship` or `drakkar` - moored on the first deep water in front of you, with new living Vikings already sitting in its seats - a full crew with `full`, `max` or nothing given, or as many as given, up to a full crew - to see where they all sit. The boat never sets off, and they sit there doing nothing else until it's broken, when they swim ashore and go their way like any Viking with no village. It drops nothing when it breaks
  - `kri_vv_spawnboat despawn` takes away every boat spawned with it within 150 meters of you, with the Vikings still on board them - leaving the boats Vikings build on the road alone, and the Vikings of a boat that broke, who are on their way by then
  - `kri_vv_spawnboat pos` says where you stand on the boat you're on - or the nearest spawned with the command within 30 meters - in meters from the ship's middle across, up and along it, the way the seats are laid out, with the nearest seat, also written to the log - `kri_vv_spawnboat pos copy` copies it to the clipboard too
- `kri_vv_destroyboats [meters] [all]` - needs `devcommands`. Takes away the Vikings' boats within 50 meters of you, or as far as given up to 1000 - those on the road, on quests and spawned with `kri_vv_spawnboat` - dropping nothing. `all` takes away every ship there instead, players' and the villages' own moored boats too, but the one you're standing on. Vikings on board end up in the water and go on as they would from any boat gone from under them, climbing onto the nearest boat or swimming for land

## Test Runs

- `kri_vv_testlog [start [spawn [farm]]|stop]` - for long tests of a village getting by on its own. `start` begins a test run of the village you're in or near - or, with no village near, of the first one set up near you, so its building and setup are in the run too, from an unexplored farm or `kri_vv_spawnvillage`. `start spawn` also spawns a village where you stand once the run has begun, the way `kri_vv_spawnvillage` does - the farm named, or one at random - so it needs devcommands, in single player or as the host, and no village near. A run writes extra lines to the log: the time on every line, why Vikings die, where they get into trouble, loot that despawns before they put it away, a snapshot of every village and Viking every 15 minutes with totals of what they did so far, and the final totals when the run ends. `stop` ends it, and so does leaving the world or closing the game. Everything Viking Villages logs during the run goes to `BepInEx\LogOutput.log` as usual, and to the run's own file in `BepInEx\VikingVillagesTestLog\`, named after the village's biome, like `Meadows.log`, then `Meadows-2.log` for the next run there, so no earlier run is written over. The totals count the quests that set out, came back or were given up on, what they brought home, and the ore and fuel put in smelters. On its own, the command says how the run is going
  - Each snapshot also lists the groups of Vikings kept on the road - where they're going, roughly where they are now, when they're due and what they can do on the road - and the news the villages have heard of each other, and the log notes every road planned, every group taken off the road, rerouted or due, and every boat built, boarded and broken up
- `kri_vv_serverrun [start|stop]` - an experiment, which needs `devcommands` and only works on a dedicated server. `start`, standing in a village, has the server run that village whenever no player is within 100 meters of it, so its Vikings carry on with nobody there, and starts a test run on the server, saved in the server's own `BepInEx\VikingVillagesTestLog\`. When a player comes near, the server lets go and their game takes over the village. `stop` ends it. It runs one village at a time, and lasts until it's stopped or the server shuts down. On its own, the command says how the run is going

## The Game's Own Commands

- `spawn Viking` spawns a living Viking - near a village they join it, and out in the wild they're lost for a little while before setting out for the nearest village (see [Living Vikings](LOGIC.md#living-vikings) and [Vikings With No Village](LOGIC.md#vikings-with-no-village) in LOGIC.md)
- With `debugmode` on, looking at a Viking shows what they're doing, and looking at a village's bonfire shows the village's state - and D at a village's bonfire shows it in a sidebar down the left of the screen instead, between your hotbar and your food, whenever you're in a village, until you press D at one again (see Living Vikings in the [README](README.md))
- The game's kill commands, and the K key in `debugmode`, only kill the living Vikings who are angry at you while your PvP is off, and every living Viking with it on
