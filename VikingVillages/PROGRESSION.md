## Which Biome Counts

- **A Viking's biome** is rolled when they first appear and kept for life - the harder of the biome they appear in and their village's biome. Everything in [Vikings by Biome](#vikings-by-biome) and [Viking Features](#viking-features) goes by it, so a Viking who moves to an easier village keeps their gear, skills and odds
- **A village's biome** is the hardest biome within 50 meters of its farm's middle - checked at the middle and in 16 directions 25 and 50 meters out, the sea counting as the Meadows - so a Meadows farm by the Black Forest has Black Forest Vikings. Its station upgrades, cooking stations, cauldron, cart, tools and quests go by it, so its stations always repair its Vikings' gear
- **The farm's own biome**, at its very middle, sets its starter food and pickaxes, shore boat, raised ground, grausten bridges, wisp lights, portal home and snow clearing - so a Meadows farm by the Black Forest has Black Forest Vikings but a Meadows village's food and boat
- A farm in or within 150 meters of any biome harder than the Meadows is built bigger

## Vikings by Biome

What a living Viking of each biome starts with. Odds are rolled once, when they're rolled up, and a Viking who comes back after dying is rolled again. Dvergr Vikings are always of the Mistlands (see [Dvergr Vikings](#dvergr-vikings)).

### Meadows Viking

- Skills of 1-12, and at least 50 max health - 115 fully fed on the Meadows' best food
- Melee hits 1.4 times as hard as yours, and fists that hit for 25
- Knows Eikthyr's power
- The village's archer carries a bow and a melee weapon, the rest one weapon of any kind
- 50% a helmet, 50% a cape, 40% a shield
- A torch - never a Dverger circlet
- No strength belt, wider pockets or deeper pockets
- Hunts boar, necks and deer (archers only), only on Meadows ground

### Black Forest Viking

- Skills of 13-25, and at least 115 max health - 155 fully fed
- Melee hits 1.7 times as hard as yours, and fists that hit for 45
- Knows Eikthyr's and the Elder's powers
- A third carry only a bow, a third only a melee weapon and a third both - the village's archer has only the bow half the time
- 50% a helmet, 50% a cape, 40% a shield
- 10% a Dverger circlet in place of a torch
- 20% a strength belt
- Adds greylings to the Meadows' prey, on ground no harder than the Black Forest

### Swamp Viking

- Skills of 26-37, and at least 155 max health - 175 fully fed
- Melee hits 1.8 times as hard as yours, and fists that hit for 75
- Knows every power up to Bonemass's
- A fifth carry only a bow, a fifth only a melee weapon and the rest both - the village's archer has only the bow a quarter of the time
- 50% a helmet, 50% a cape, 40% a shield
- 30% a Dverger circlet in place of a torch
- 40% a strength belt
- Adds draugr and leeches (archers only) to their prey

### Mountains Viking

- Skills of 38-50, and at least 175 max health - 195 fully fed
- Melee hits 1.75 times as hard as yours, and fists that hit for 95
- Knows every power up to Moder's
- Always both a melee weapon and a bow, always their set's helmet and a cape that resists frost, and 40% a shield
- 50% a Dverger circlet in place of a torch
- 60% a strength belt
- 20% wider pockets
- With Zombie Apocalypse, 25% silver-lined weapons
- Adds wolves and drakes (archers only) to their prey

### Plains Viking

- Skills of 50-62, and at least 195 max health - 235 fully fed
- Melee hits 1.25 times as hard as yours, and fists that hit for 110
- Knows every power up to Yagluth's
- Always both a melee weapon and a bow - the Mountains' Draugr Fang, since the Plains have none - always their set's helmet and a cape, and 40% a shield
- 70% a Dverger circlet in place of a torch
- 80% a strength belt
- 40% wider pockets
- With Zombie Apocalypse, 50% silver-lined weapons
- Adds lox and deathsquitos (archers only) to their prey, and hunts as a party of 3-4 for anything an archer doesn't hunt alone

### Mistlands Viking

- Skills of 63-75, and at least 235 max health - 270 fully fed
- Melee hits as hard as yours, and fists that hit for 125
- Knows every power up to the Queen's
- Always both a melee weapon and a bow or crossbow, always their set's helmet and a cape, and 40% a shield
- Always a Dverger circlet, and a wisp light worn on their belt against the mist
- Always a strength belt, carried in their pack, since the wisp light takes its slot - worn for a quest outside the Mistlands
- 60% wider pockets, and 20% deeper pockets as well
- With Zombie Apocalypse, 75% silver-lined weapons
- Adds hares to their prey, hunting as a party

### Ashlands Viking

- Skills of 76-87, and at least 270 max health - 325 fully fed
- Melee hits as hard as yours, and fists that hit for 145
- Knows every power, up to Fader's
- Always both a melee weapon and a bow or crossbow, always their set's helmet and a cape, and 40% a shield
- Always a Dverger circlet
- Always a strength belt, worn
- 80% wider pockets, and 40% deeper pockets as well
- With Zombie Apocalypse, always silver-lined weapons
- Adds Asksvin and voltures (archers only) to their prey, hunting as a party

### Deep North Viking

- Skills of 88-100, and at least 325 max health - 350 fully fed
- Melee hits as hard as yours, and fists that hit for 165
- Knows every power, as in the Ashlands - the Deep North has no boss
- Always both a melee weapon and a bow or crossbow - never Dyrnwyn - always their set's helmet and a cape that resists frost, and 40% a shield
- Always a Dverger circlet
- Always a strength belt, worn
- Always wider pockets, and 60% deeper pockets as well
- With Zombie Apocalypse, always silver-lined weapons
- Adds moose and seals to their prey, hunting as a party

### Dvergr Vikings

Always rolled as Mistlands Vikings - the Mistlands' skills and gear, both weapons, always a Dverger circlet and a wisp light, and 75% silver-lined weapons with Zombie Apocalypse - but with a Mistlands zombie Viking's health of 650 times their stars, and no strength belt or pockets. Like a living Mistlands Viking, they know every power up to the Queen's.

A strength belt (Megingjord) raises how much a Viking carries from 300 to 450, the way it does yours. In the Mistlands it goes in their pack rather than their utility slot, which their wisp light takes, and they put it on for a quest that goes outside the Mistlands. From the Black Forest on, a village buys one from Haldor for a Viking without one.

## Villages by Biome

What a village of each biome is built and stocked with. Every village has a workbench, a forge, a bonfire with a cooking station over it, 3 axes in storage and a stack each of wood, stone and resin to start with.

### Meadows Village

- A level 2 workbench and a level 1 forge
- Starts with 10 cooked boar meat and 10 raspberries, and no pickaxes
- Wooden cooking stations, no cauldron and no cart
- A raft by the water
- Never goes mining

### Black Forest Village

- A level 3 workbench and a level 1 forge
- Starts with 10 cooked deer meat, 10 blueberries and 2 antler pickaxes
- Wooden cooking stations, a cauldron with no extensions, and a cart
- Keeps 4 pickaxes and an axe that cuts fine wood in storage
- A karve by the water

### Swamp Village

- A level 3 workbench and a level 2 forge
- Starts with 10 deer stew, 10 cooked fish and 2 bronze pickaxes
- Iron cooking stations, a cauldron with a spice rack, and a cart
- Keeps 4 pickaxes and an axe that cuts fine wood
- Its ground is raised out of the swamp water
- A karve by the water

### Mountains Village

- A level 3 workbench and a level 3 forge
- Starts with 10 sausages, 10 onion soup and 2 iron pickaxes
- Iron cooking stations, a cauldron with 2 extensions, and a cart
- Keeps 4 pickaxes and an axe that cuts fine wood
- A longship by the water

### Plains Village

- A level 3 workbench and a level 4 forge
- Starts with 10 cooked lox meat, 10 bread and 2 iron pickaxes
- Iron cooking stations, a cauldron with 3 extensions, and a cart
- Keeps 4 pickaxes and an axe that cuts fine wood
- Its ground is raised out of any water it stands in
- A longship by the water

### Mistlands Village

- A level 3 workbench and a level 4 forge
- Starts with 10 cooked hare meat, 10 cooked bug meat and 2 black metal pickaxes
- Iron cooking stations, a cauldron with 4 extensions, and a cart
- Keeps 4 pickaxes and an axe that cuts fine wood
- Lit with wisp torches and a wisp fountain against the mist
- A longship by the water

### Ashlands Village

- A level 3 workbench, a level 4 forge, and any black forge or galdr table its houses came with upgraded to level 4 and level 2
- Starts with 10 cooked Asksvin meat, 10 cooked volture meat and 2 black metal pickaxes
- Iron cooking stations, a cauldron with 5 extensions, and no cart
- Keeps 4 pickaxes and an axe that cuts fine wood
- Grausten bridges, its paths going around the lava and the boiling sea
- A stone portal home to an easier village
- A drakkar by the water

### Deep North Village

- A level 3 workbench, a level 4 forge, and any black forge or galdr table its houses came with upgraded to level 4
- Starts with 10 cooked moose meat, 10 cooked seal blubber and 2 black metal pickaxes
- Iron cooking stations, a cauldron with all 6 extensions, and a cart
- Keeps 4 pickaxes and an axe that cuts fine wood
- Grausten bridges
- A stone portal home to an easier village
- Its Vikings clear the heavy snow off its pieces and the deep snow off its ground
- A drakkar by the water

### Buying From Haldor

Each at most once every 7 game days, when the village's coins and treasure will pay for it - Haldor's own price, read from his list. The Viking who lacks it goes, and comes home wearing it or with the pockets sewn in. Unlike you, they don't need to have beaten Moder for wider pockets or the Queen for deeper ones.

| Purchase | Biome from | Price | For a Viking with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dverger circlet | Black Forest | 620 | Only torches |
| Strength belt | Black Forest | 950 | No strength belt, while the chests hold no spare one |
| Wider pockets | Mountains | 1000 | No pockets |
| Deeper pockets | Mistlands | 2000 | Wider pockets only |
